# "I Saved You a Seat": shot list and what each shot stands for

An animated concept trailer for SameSky, 2:00, 1920×1080, 24 fps. Every shot is 3D animation rendered from the Unity
project for this film ([how it was made](README.md)). **None of it is footage of the running app.** This list says, shot
by shot, what the film shows and what that corresponds to in SameSky as it stands today.

How to read the last column:

- **In the app** — the feature exists; where a simulator or automated check covers it, that is named.
- **In the app, staged** — the feature exists, and the film shows it in a simpler or more theatrical way than the app does.
- **Concept** — the film's own image; the app does not do this as shown.

## What the film deliberately does not show

- No AI recap. The app's AI-arranged recap exists but was not verified live for this build, so the film shows only the
  moments themselves.
- No "teleporting" of footage into 3D, and no reconstruction of a place from a video: her clip stays on a screen.
- No real person. Adam and Maya are fictional; their figures are stock rigged characters recoloured for the film, and
  their voices are text-to-speech.

## Continuity notes for anyone re-cutting it

- The cup is the same one throughout: it is set down in s01, waits in s09 and s11, is lifted in s12, and stands on its
  coaster from s13b on.
- Screen direction in the lounge: Adam is always on the left of a profile shot and Maya on the right; the window is
  behind Maya, the alcove behind the table.
- Maya's cardigan and Adam's jacket never change; the light does (afternoon in s01, evening for his first visit, dusk
  for the last beat).
